Travel Guide

How to get to Mangalore, India

For our guests flying in, we recommend booking your primary flight to Kempegowda International Airport (BLR) in Bengaluru. From there, you can catch a quick, direct domestic flight into Mangaluru International Airport (IXE).

Travel Tip: Direct flights into Mangaluru (IXE) are also frequently available from other major transit hubs like Mumbai (BOM) and New Delhi (DEL).

Explore Mangalore

If you have some free time during/after the wedding weekend, we highly recommend exploring the area! The coastal region is famous for a few things you definitely shouldn't miss:

Pristine Beaches

Spend a relaxing morning by the water. Tannirbhavi Beach and Panambur Beach are local favorites, offering gorgeous golden sands and peaceful coastal views.

Incredible Local Food

Mangalore is an absolute paradise for food lovers. Be sure to try the famous Mangalorean seafood, Chicken Ghee Roast, and soft Neer Dosas at local spots like Machali or Giri Manja's.

Culture & Cafes

Visit the stunning St. Aloysius Chapel, known for its beautiful frescoes, or explore the ancient coastal temples. And for a quick pick-me-up, grab a cup of local filter coffee or find a cozy cafe to enjoy a great morning latte.

Shopping for Traditional Attire

If you're looking to buy Indian wear for the events, here are a few great areas to explore!

Bangalore

Commercial Street: Endless options for lehengas, kurtas, and jewelry at every price point.

Jayanagar: The perfect area to find traditional silk sarees and authentic South Indian attire.

Indiranagar: Great for high-end designer boutiques and modern fusion wear.

Mangalore

KS Rao Road & Hampankatta: The main local hub for traditional clothing stores, trusted textile shops, and tailoring.

City Centre Mall: Great for ready-to-wear ethnic brands and convenient, all-in-one shopping.

Questions & Answers

A few helpful answers as you plan your trip and get ready for the celebrations. We'll keep updating this list as more questions pop up :)

Do I need a visa to attend the wedding?

Most international guests, including U.S. citizens, will need a visa to enter India. We recommend applying for an e-Tourist Visa online and checking the latest requirements for your citizenship before applying. It’s best to apply well in advance of your travel dates as processing times can vary. Please reach out to us if you have any questions about the visa process!

How do I pay while in India?

Visa and Mastercard are accepted at most hotels, restaurants, and larger businesses. Carrying cash is still useful for small shops and local vendors. It’d be a good idea to convert some USD to INR at the Banaglore airport.

Where will I be staying, and how do I get to the venues?

We are happily taking care of all room bookings and transportation to the venues in Mangalore for our guests! Please share your travel itinerary with us as soon as possible so we can finalize your accommodations and coordinate your rides.
